Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Qalculyn, privacy, and architecture.

Does Qalculyn collect or transmit any user data?
No. Qalculyn does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. All mathematical calculations, history logs, and custom study notes reside exclusively on your physical device.
What is the application package ID on Google Play?
The official application ID is com.sws.qalculyn, published by Spring Web Solutions.
What Android versions are supported?
Qalculyn supports Android 5.0 (API level 21) and newer, including the latest Android 14 and Android 15 releases with full 64-bit ARM and x86 hardware acceleration.

You can view our dedicated, Google Play-compliant privacy policy at qalculyn.springwebsolutions.in/privacy.
