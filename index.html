Privacy Policy for Qalculyn

Effective Date: October 15, 2026 | Last Updated: October 15, 2026

Qalculyn (“the app”), developed and published by Spring Web Solutions (“we”, “us”, or “our”), is designed as a privacy-first, 100% offline mathematics and calculation utility. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Qalculyn does not collect, harvest, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require accounts, logins, emails, contact lists, or phone numbers.

2. 100% Offline Functionality

Qalculyn operates entirely offline. The application does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission in its Android manifest. All mathematical calculations, solver operations, and user study notes execute and remain exclusively on your local device.

3. Device Storage and Local Data

  • Calculation History & Notes: Stored strictly in an app-scoped local SQLite database on your device.
  • User Preferences: Display modes and color theme selections are saved locally via Android SharedPreferences.
  • You can clear all stored calculations at any time from within the app settings or via Android system settings.

4. No Advertisements or Third-Party Analytics

Qalculyn contains no third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or diagnostic trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). Your usage data is neither tracked nor monetized.

5. Permissions Audit

Qalculyn requests zero sensitive device permissions. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, files, or contacts.

6. Children’s Privacy

Because the app collects no user data whatsoever, it is fully compliant with COPPA, GDPR, and Google Play Families Policies.
