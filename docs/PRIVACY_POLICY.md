# Privacy Policy for Qalculyn

**Effective Date:** October 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 15, 2026  

Qalculyn ("we", "our", or "the app") is developed as a privacy-first, 100% offline mathematics and calculation utility. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to user privacy and explains how data is handled.

---

### 1. Zero Personal Data Collection
Qalculyn does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal identifiable information (PII). We do not require registration, accounts, email addresses, phone numbers, or passwords.

### 2. 100% Offline Functionality
- Qalculyn does **not request internet permission** (`android.permission.INTERNET` is not included in the application manifest).
- All calculations, mathematical algorithms, history logs, and custom user notes are processed and stored exclusively on your local device.
- No network connections are initiated, and no telemetry, crash logs, or analytics are sent to external servers or third parties.

### 3. Device Storage and Local Data
- **Calculation History & Notes:** Saved strictly on the local SQLite database on your device using Android app-scoped internal storage.
- **Preferences:** Theme mode, accent color, and display settings are stored locally using Android SharedPreferences.
- You can clear your calculation history and stored data at any time directly within the app settings, or by clearing app data in your Android device settings.

### 4. Third-Party Services & Advertisements
- Qalculyn contains **no third-party advertising SDKs**.
- Qalculyn contains **no third-party tracking or analytics SDKs** (such as Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, or Facebook SDK).
- Qalculyn does not sell, rent, or monetize your data.

### 5. Permissions
Qalculyn requests zero sensitive Android permissions:
- No Camera access
- No Microphone access
- No Location access
- No Contacts access
- No External Storage access
- No Network or Internet access

### 6. Children's Privacy
Because Qalculyn does not collect any personal data from anyone, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policies.

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we make updates to this policy in future versions, the changes will be reflected with an updated "Last Updated" date on this page.
