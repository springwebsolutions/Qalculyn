## 1. Core Calculator & Scientific Keypad

The main calculator provides clean, instantaneous mathematical evaluations with real-time previewing.

### Keypad Ergonomics & Gestures
- **Interactive Tap-to-Edit Cursor:** Tap anywhere inside an active equation to place a cursor. You can insert missing digits or operators right in the middle without deleting succeeding numbers.
- **Swipe-to-Delete Gesture:** Swipe left or right horizontally across the calculation display to delete the previous character.
- **Physical Keyboard & Numpad:** Full support for external USB and Bluetooth keyboards (`Enter` = `=`, `Esc` = `C`, arrows for cursor navigation).
- **Quick Tools Bar:** One-tap shortcut chips located right above the keypad for instant access to Constants, Simultaneous Equations, Polynomial Division, Geometry, Graphing, and GST.
- **Live Preview:** While typing an equation (e.g. `125 × 8 + 45`), the computed preview appears below the input before pressing `=`.
- **Long Press to Copy:** Long-press the primary result to copy the formatted number directly to your clipboard.
- **Auto-Closing Parentheses:** Pressing `=` automatically closes unclosed parentheses without returning syntax errors.
- **Calculation History:** Tap the **Clock Icon** in the top bar to inspect past calculations. Tap any history item to reload its expression or copy its result.

### Airtight Sign Toggling ($\pm$)
The `±` key is designed to wrap and unwrap negative values naturally:
- **Single number:** `15` $\rightarrow$ `(-15)` $\rightarrow$ `15`
- **Compound expression:** `25 + 8` $\rightarrow$ `25 + (-8)` $\rightarrow$ `25 + 8`
- **After an operator:** If you type `100 ×` and press `±`, the display enters `100 × (-`. If you change your mind and press `±` again, it returns cleanly to `100 ×` without leaving stray brackets on the right.

### Step-by-Step Expression Explanations & Notes
- **Inline Explain Button:** Whenever you type or calculate an expression, an **`[✦ Explain]`** button appears directly beneath the display line.
- **Top Bar Explain Button:** Tap the **`✦`** (Auto Awesome) button on the top-right toolbar to open the step-by-step breakdown modal at any time.
- **History Step Breakdown:** Every past calculation in your History sheet has an individual **`✦`** explain action button.
- **Pure Mathematical Steps:** Follows strict PEMDAS / BODMAS evaluation order without distracting conversational jargon.
- **Custom Explanation Note Editor:** Tap **"Add / Edit Custom Explanation"** inside the step sheet to write, save, or edit personal study notes and problem reminders.

### Scientific Functions
Tap the **Scientific Toggle (fx)** in the top bar to expand advanced functions:
- **Trigonometric:** `sin`, `cos`, `tan` (supports degrees or radians via Settings)
- **Inverse & Hyperbolic:** `asin`, `acos`, `atan`, `sinh`, `cosh`, `tanh`
- **Logarithms & Powers:** Natural log (`ln`), base-10 log (`log`), exponential (`e^x`), square root ($\sqrt{x}$), cube root ($\sqrt[3]{x}$), powers ($x^y$)
- **Combinatorics:** Permutations (`nPr`), combinations (`nCr`), and factorials (`!`)

## 2. Everyday & Consumer Tools

Access all tools by tapping the **Grid Icon** in the top bar or swiping to the Tools screen.

### Percentage
- Calculate percentages of values (e.g., *What is 15% of 850?*).
- Calculate percentage increases/decreases (e.g., *From 50 to 75 is a 50% increase*).

## 4. Number Theory & Algebra

### LCM & GCD / HCF
- Calculate Least Common Multiple (LCM) and Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) for arbitrary numbers.
- View Euclidean algorithm step-by-step division chains.

### Modular Arithmetic & Extended Euclidean Suite
- **Bézout's Identity ($ax + by = \gcd(a, b)$):** Calculates integer coefficients $x$ and $y$ with both forward division steps and backward substitution derivations.
- **Modular Exponentiation ($a^b \pmod m$):** Computes large modular powers efficiently using repeated binary squaring with step derivations.
- **Modular Multiplicative Inverse ($a^{-1} \pmod m$):** Finds $x$ such that $(a \cdot x) \equiv 1 \pmod m$. Verifies the coprimality condition $\gcd(a, m) = 1$.
- **Euler's Totient Function ($\phi(n)$):** Computes the count of coprime positive integers up to $n$ with prime factorization product formula derivations.

### Prime Numbers & Factorization
- Instant primality check using trial division and 6k $\pm$ 1 optimization.
- View complete prime factorizations (e.g., $360 = 2^3 \times 3^2 \times 5$) and full divisor sets.

### Exact Fractions & Ratios
- Performs arithmetic on fractions without converting to lossy floating-point decimals.
- **Step-by-Step Arithmetic Derivations:** Tap "View Step-by-Step Derivation" to see denominator LCM computation, scaling to equivalent fractions, numerator combination, and GCD simplification.
- **Simplification Breakdown:** Displays prime factorization cancellation steps for numerators and denominators.
- Simplifies ratios of arbitrary numbers (e.g., `12 : 18 : 24` $\rightarrow$ `2 : 3 : 4`).

### Algebra & Equation Solvers
- **Simultaneous Linear Equations (2x2 & 3x3):** Solves systems of 2 or 3 linear equations using Cramer's Rule, Gaussian Elimination, and Substitution with exact fractions. Identifies unique, infinite, or impossible cases.
- **Polynomial Long Division & Synthetic Division:** Divides polynomial $P(x)$ by $D(x)$ to obtain quotient $Q(x)$ and remainder $R(x)$ with a step-by-step division tableau.
- **Quadratic Solver ($ax^2 + bx + c = 0$):** Analytical solutions over real and complex roots ($x_1, x_2$).
- **Linear Solver ($ax + b = cx + d$):** Solves for single solutions, identifies identities (infinite solutions), or contradictions (no solution).
- **Polynomial Evaluator:** Evaluates polynomial $P(x)$ at any specified point $x = c$.
- **Personal Explanation Notes:** Under the analytical steps, tap **"Add Explanation"** to save custom study notes, reminders, or formulas.

## 5. Advanced STEM & Calculus

### Scientific & Physical Constants Catalog
- Search over 40 fundamental physical and chemical constants (CODATA standard) categorized into Universal, Electromagnetism, Atomic & Nuclear, Thermodynamics, and Astronomy.
- View exact values and SI units, copy values with one tap, or insert constants directly into the main calculator equation.

### Calculus & Limits
- **Derivative $f'(x)$:** Computes instantaneous slopes using a 5-point central stencil algorithm.
- **Definite Integral $\int_a^b f(x)\,dx$:** Numerical quadrature using Composite Simpson's 1/3 rule.
- **Limits $\lim_{x \to c} f(x)$:** Evaluates two-sided, left-hand ($x \to c^-$), and right-hand ($x \to c^+$) limits.

### Matrices & 3D Vectors
- **Row Reduction (RREF):** Performs Gauss-Jordan elimination on matrices of arbitrary dimensions. Tap "View Row Operation Steps" to see every row swap, scale, and elimination step.
- **Eigenvalues (2×2 and 3×3):** Computes characteristic polynomials, traces, determinants, and exact real or complex eigenvalues.
- **Matrix Operations:** Computes determinants, matrix inverses, additions, subtractions, and matrix products.
- **3D Vector Geometry:** Evaluates dot products, cross products, magnitudes, unit vectors ($\hat{u}$), and angles in degrees and radians.

### Complex Numbers ($\mathbb{C}$)
- Enter complex numbers as rectangular coordinates ($a + bi$) or polar ($r, \theta$).
- Computes modulus $|z|$, argument $\text{Arg}(z)$, conjugate $z^*$, powers $z^n$, and square roots $\sqrt{z}$.
- Features the **Custom Study Note** button for personal reminders and notes.

### Statistics, Regression & Probability
- **Two-Variable Linear Regression ($y = mx + c$):** Computes the best-fit line, slope $m$, intercept $c$, Pearson correlation $r$, and determination $R^2$ with an interactive scatter plot canvas and 7-step derivation modal.
- **Descriptive Metrics:** Mean, median, sample variance ($s^2$), population variance ($\sigma^2$), first quartile ($Q_1$), third quartile ($Q_3$), and interquartile range (IQR).
- **Exact Combinatorics:** Evaluates $n!$, $nPr$, and $nCr$ with arbitrary-precision BigInt integers.
- **Normal Distribution:** Computes probability density $f(x)$ and cumulative probability $P(X \le x)$ for arbitrary mean $\mu$ and standard deviation $\sigma$.

## 6. Interactive Geometry Studio & Vector Canvas

The Geometry Studio brings figures to life with custom vector drawings and inverse mathematical solvers.

### Supported Shapes
- **16 2D Figures:** Circle, Circular Sector, Annulus (Ring), Ellipse, Rectangle, Square, Parallelogram, Rhombus, Trapezoid, Triangle, Equilateral Triangle, Regular Pentagon, Hexagon, Octagon, Regular $n$-gon, and Kite.
- **14 3D Solids:** Sphere, Hemisphere, Spherical Cap, Cylinder, Hollow Cylinder, Cone, Frustum, Rectangular Prism, Cube, Square Pyramid, Regular Tetrahedron, Ellipsoid, Torus, and Triangular Prism.
- **Analytic Triangles:** Solves complete triangles from SSS (3 sides), SAS (Side-Angle-Side), ASA (Angle-Side-Angle), or AAS.

### Inverse Solvers
Find dimensions from known area, perimeter, volume, or surface area:
- Missing radius from known circle area or circumference
- Missing cylinder height from known volume and radius
- Missing sphere radius from volume or surface area
- Missing cube edge from space diagonal

### Vector Canvas Features
- Vector illustrations scale dynamically to entered dimensions.
- 3D solids feature isometric wireframe perspectives with highlighted dimensional labels.
- Tap **"Export Geometry Study Sheet"** to generate a Markdown summary with all formulas copied to your clipboard.
